# Benchmark 001 — Coffee explainer (Codex)

## Task to give Codex
Use the MotionLab skill to make a 15-second, 1080x1920, 60 FPS cinematic explainer in Indonesian about how coffee beans become espresso. Audience: curious beginners. Use an original 3-shot narrative: bean, extraction, espresso. Do not reuse the internet/router/globe imagery. Use a cohesive visual language and a legible headline per shot.

You may create or generate new visual assets and extend the scene graph/React components when necessary. Follow the skill's storyboard, asset, motion and visual QA references. Start Remotion Studio early. Preview and inspect representative frames, then iterate. Run typecheck. Do not export MP4 until explicitly requested. Report what was visually inspected and what remains unverified.

## Acceptance criteria
- Script clearly explains a causal sequence and matches the visuals.
- No router, phone, globe, or generic network imagery.
- Assets depict coffee beans, extraction, and espresso rather than renamed placeholders.
- Three shots have intentional camera direction, entrance, readable hold and exit.
- All scenes work in standalone preview and the full composition.
- Typecheck passes; visual review includes opening, middle and ending frames.
- Agent documents any generated asset source and does not claim unverified preview success.

## Evaluation rubric (0–5 each)
1. Story clarity and factual accuracy.
2. Asset-to-narration fidelity.
3. Composition, lighting and typographic readability.
4. Choreography and camera intentionality.
5. Technical reliability and honest QA evidence.

Passing threshold: >=20/25, with no category below 3. This is an evaluation target, not a claimed result.
